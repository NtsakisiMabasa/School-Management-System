ABOUT SCHOOLCONNECT

Building connected school communities.

SchoolConnect is a digital platform designed to improve communication between students, teachers and schools.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to make school information easier to access while creating a stronger connection between students, teachers and administrators.

💡

Our Vision

We envision schools where technology makes learning, communication and student support more accessible.

HOW IT WORKS

One platform. Multiple possibilities.

01

Create an account

Register using your school information and create your SchoolConnect profile.

02

Connect to your school

Access your school's announcements, resources, assignments and timetable.

03

Stay connected

Communicate with your school community and stay updated throughout the academic year.
